About Court10

Made
to Move

Built from the baseline up, Court10 creates racket sports apparel driven by precision, performance, and a refusal to settle.

No borrowed gymwear, no gimmicks. Just sleek, purpose-built kit designed to move with the game.

Engineered for the players, we're an independent company founded in the UK & Ireland. We're here for the grassroots enthusiasts and the rallies that go into the night. For the professionals scaling unscaled heights.
